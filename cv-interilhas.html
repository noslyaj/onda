About CV Interilhas

CV Interilhas operates the largest fleet of inter-island ferries in Cape Verde. The company connects all 9 inhabited islands through three main service lines, using a fleet that includes the Chiquinho, HSC Liberdadi, and Kriola vessels.

The company is based in Mindelo, São Vicente, and its official website is cvinterilhas.cv. Tickets can be purchased online, at port offices, or through local travel agents across the islands.

CV Interilhas Routes

Linha Barlavento

The Barlavento line is the most frequent service, connecting São Vicente and Santo Antão with daily crossings. The vessel Chiquinho operates this route with departures from Mindelo at 07:00 and 14:00 and returns from Porto Novo at 09:00 and 16:00. The crossing takes approximately 60 minutes.

Linha Sotavento

The Sotavento line connects the southern islands using the vessel Kriola. It serves Santiago, Fogo, Brava and Maio on a rotating weekly schedule. Santiago to Fogo takes approximately 4 hours. Santiago to Maio takes approximately 2 hours. The Fogo to Brava crossing is the shortest at around 1 hour.

Linha Redonda

The Redonda line is the longest route, connecting São Vicente with São Nicolau, Sal, Boa Vista and Santiago using the HSC Liberdadi. This circular route operates twice weekly and journey times range from 3 hours for the shortest leg to 19 hours for the full São Vicente to Santiago crossing.

Buying CV Interilhas Tickets

Tickets can be purchased through the CV Interilhas website at cvinterilhas.cv/buy, at port ticket offices, or through authorised travel agents on each island.

There are two price tiers: a national rate for Cape Verdean residents and a non-national rate for visitors. The non-national rate is typically around 60 percent higher than the national rate. Prices shown on Ondas are the non-national fare unless otherwise noted.

For the Barlavento line, tickets can usually be purchased on the day of travel at the port. For the Sotavento and Redonda lines, booking in advance is recommended as these routes operate less frequently and can sell out.

CV Interilhas Cancellations and Delays

Ferry services can be disrupted by adverse sea conditions, particularly during the windier months from November to March. When the Instituto Nacional de Meteorologia e Geofísica (INMG) issues weather warnings, CV Interilhas may delay or cancel sailings.

The Redonda and longer Sotavento routes are most susceptible to weather-related disruptions. The short Barlavento crossing between São Vicente and Santo Antão is the most reliable and rarely gets cancelled. Always check close to your travel date for the latest status.

Common Questions About CV Interilhas

Does CV Interilhas connect all 9 islands?

Yes. Through its three service lines (Barlavento, Sotavento and Redonda), CV Interilhas provides connections to all 9 inhabited islands of Cape Verde: São Vicente, Santo Antão, São Nicolau, Sal, Boa Vista, Santiago, Maio, Fogo and Brava.

How far in advance can I book a CV Interilhas ticket?

Tickets typically become available for purchase once the seasonal schedule is published. For popular routes, booking a few days ahead is advisable. For the Barlavento line you can often buy at the port on the day.

What vessels does CV Interilhas operate?

CV Interilhas currently operates three main vessels: the Chiquinho on the Barlavento line, the Kriola on the Sotavento line, and the HSC Liberdadi on the Redonda line.

Is there a difference in price for nationals and non-nationals?

Yes. CV Interilhas offers a reduced fare for Cape Verdean nationals and residents. The non-national fare is approximately 60 percent higher. You may be asked to show identification when boarding.

Can I take luggage on a CV Interilhas ferry?

Yes. Passengers can bring luggage onboard. Large items and excess baggage may incur an additional charge. Vehicles can also be transported on certain routes — check with the operator for availability and pricing.
