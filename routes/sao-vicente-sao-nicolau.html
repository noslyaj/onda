São Vicente to São Nicolau Ferry

Search São Vicente → São Nicolau ferries arrow_forward

The ferry from São Vicente to São Nicolau connects the port of Mindelo with Ribeira Brava on São Nicolau. The crossing takes approximately 3 hours and runs twice weekly as part of the Linha Redonda circuit operated by CV Interilhas.

The Route

Ferries depart from the terminal in Mindelo, São Vicente's lively port city, and arrive at the harbour in Ribeira Brava on the southern coast of São Nicolau. The route passes through open Atlantic waters between the Barlavento islands.

How Long Does the Crossing Take?

The journey from São Vicente to São Nicolau takes approximately 3 hours.

The Vessel

The route is served by the Dona Tututa, a high-speed catamaran operated by CV Interilhas on the Linha Redonda. This vessel also connects the other northern islands in the Barlavento group.

How Often Do Ferries Run?

The São Vicente to São Nicolau ferry runs twice weekly. Schedules can shift seasonally, so always check the latest sailing times on Ondas before planning your trip.

About São Nicolau

São Nicolau is a quiet, mountainous island known for the Monte Gordo Natural Park, dramatic ribeiras, and a slower pace of life compared to São Vicente. The island is a wonderful destination for hikers and travellers looking to experience authentic Cape Verdean culture away from the tourist trail. Ribeira Brava, the main town, has a charming colonial centre with colourful buildings and a relaxed atmosphere.

Practical Tips

The open-water crossing can be choppy, especially in the winter months when trade winds are stronger. Bring seasickness medication if you are sensitive to motion. The catamaran has indoor seating with air conditioning. Arrive at the Mindelo terminal at least 30 minutes before departure to complete boarding.
