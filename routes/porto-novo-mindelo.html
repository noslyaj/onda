Porto Novo to Mindelo Ferry

Search Porto Novo → Mindelo ferries arrow_forward

The ferry from Porto Novo on Santo Antão back to Mindelo on São Vicente runs throughout the day and is the return leg of the most popular ferry crossing in Cape Verde. Whether you have spent a day hiking in the valleys or a few nights exploring the island, this crossing brings you back to the transport hub of the northern archipelago.

The Route

Ferries depart from the port of Porto Novo on Santo Antão and arrive at the Cais Marítima terminal in Mindelo on São Vicente. The crossing takes approximately 60 minutes.

How Often Do Ferries Run?

Multiple sailings run every day in both directions. CV Interilhas and Nôs Ferry both operate on this route, giving you more flexibility than almost any other crossing in Cape Verde. Use the Ondas search tool to see the full list of departures on your chosen date.

Arriving in Mindelo

Mindelo is the cultural capital of Cape Verde and one of the most vibrant cities in the archipelago. From the terminal you can walk to the city centre or take a taxi. The city is known for its music scene, its colonial architecture and its restaurants.

Connections From Mindelo

From São Vicente you can connect onward to São Nicolau by ferry and to other islands by air. Mindelo's airport Cesária Évora serves domestic routes. Check the Ondas search tool to plan your onward journey.
