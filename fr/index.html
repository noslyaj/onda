About

Ondas is the easiest way to find ferry schedules between the islands of Cape Verde. Search any route across all 9 islands, compare departure times from CV Interilhas and Nôs Ferry, and plan your island hopping trip in seconds. Free, no account needed, works offline.

Ondas est une application web gratuite qui fonctionne dans n'importe quel navigateur sur n'importe quel téléphone. Aucun téléchargement nécessaire. Ouvrez simplement ondas.cv et recherchez.

Ondas was created by a member of the Cabo Verde diaspora as a way to give something back to the country.

Finding accurate ferry times in Cabo Verde has always been harder than it should be. Operator websites are difficult to navigate on a phone, schedules shift with the seasons, and there was nowhere to compare options across operators in one place.

Ondas changes that. Real schedules from CV Interilhas and Nôs Ferry, the two main operators serving all 9 islands, brought together in one clean and fast mobile app. Free to use, no account needed, works offline.
