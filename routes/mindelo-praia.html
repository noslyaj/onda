Mindelo to Praia Ferry

Search Mindelo → Praia ferries arrow_forward

The ferry from Mindelo on São Vicente to Praia on Santiago is one of the longest crossings in Cape Verde. It connects the cultural capital of the northern islands with the national capital in the south. Most travellers who make this journey do so on the overnight Linha Redonda service operated by CV Interilhas.

The Route

Ferries depart from the Cais Marítima terminal in Mindelo and arrive at the port in Praia on the island of Santiago. The crossing covers a significant distance across the Atlantic and takes the vessel through some of the most open water in the archipelago.

How Long Does the Crossing Take?

The journey from Mindelo to Praia takes approximately 18 to 19 hours. This is one of the longest ferry crossings in Cape Verde. Most passengers travel overnight and sleep on board.

When Do Ferries Run?

The Mindelo to Praia connection runs as part of the CV Interilhas Linha Redonda circuit, which departs twice per week. The schedule can change seasonally so always check current times before booking. Use the Ondas search tool to see the next available sailing.

Should I Fly Instead?

For many travellers the answer is yes. CV Sky operates flights between São Vicente and Santiago that take under an hour. If your time is limited, flying is the more practical option. The ferry is worth considering if you enjoy the sea crossing experience or if you are travelling with luggage and vehicles.

Buying Tickets

Tickets for the Linha Redonda can be bought online through the CV Interilhas website or at the terminal in Mindelo. Book in advance as this service runs only twice per week and can fill up during busy periods.

What to Expect on Board

The HSC Liberdadi vessel used on this route has passenger seating and basic onboard facilities. For an overnight crossing it is worth bringing your own food and a blanket or light layer as the air conditioning can be strong.

Arriving in Praia

The ferry port in Praia is located in the Plateau area of the city. Taxis and aluguers are available at the port. The city centre is a short ride away.
