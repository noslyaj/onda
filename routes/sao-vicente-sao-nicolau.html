São Vicente to São Nicolau: The Ferry to Cape Verde's Hidden Gem

Search São Vicente → São Nicolau ferries arrow_forward

The ferry from São Vicente to São Nicolau is one of the most scenic crossings in the Barlavento islands. It connects Mindelo, Cape Verde's cultural capital, with the quiet mountainous island of São Nicolau — a destination that feels genuinely off the beaten path. The route is operated by CV Interilhas as part of the Linha Redonda circuit, and it runs roughly twice a week.

The Route

The ferry departs from the terminal in Mindelo on São Vicente and arrives at the port of Preguiça on São Nicolau's southern coast. From Preguiça, shared minivans (aluguers) run to Ribeira Brava, the island's main town, about 15 minutes inland.

The crossing follows open Atlantic waters between the two islands. Conditions are generally calm between April and October but can be rougher in the winter months when trade winds pick up.

How Long Does the Crossing Take?

The ferry from São Vicente to São Nicolau takes approximately 5 to 6 hours, depending on sea conditions and the specific routing. As part of the Redonda circuit, the vessel may call at intermediate stops on some sailings, which affects total journey time. Check the Ondas search for the exact duration of each sailing.

How Often Does the Ferry Run?

The São Vicente to São Nicolau ferry runs roughly twice a week. The exact departure days vary because the service is part of the Redonda and Retangular circuit rotations, which alternate on a fortnightly cycle. An occasional third sailing may appear every two weeks when the Rectangular routing includes this leg.

Because departure days shift with the rotation, always check the live schedule on Ondas before planning your trip. Do not rely on fixed day-of-week assumptions.

Which Operator Serves This Route?

CV Interilhas is the only operator on this route. The vessel is the Dona Tututa, which serves the full Linha Redonda circuit connecting São Vicente, São Nicolau, Sal, Boa Vista and Santiago.

Nôs Ferry does not operate on this route — it serves the São Vicente to Santo Antão crossing only.

Buying Tickets

Tickets can be purchased online at cvinterilhas.cv or at the CV Interilhas ticket office in Mindelo's ferry terminal. Because this route runs only twice a week, availability can be limited during holidays and peak travel periods. Booking in advance is strongly recommended.

The non-resident fare is 2,850 CVE (~€26) per person each way. Cape Verdean residents pay a reduced fare of 1,900 CVE (~€17). Prices are set by government regulation and are the same in both directions.

Arriving at the Port

In Mindelo, the ferry terminal (Cais Marítima) is centrally located on the waterfront, walkable from most hotels and the city centre. Arrive at least 30 minutes before departure. Boarding closes before the scheduled time and the vessel will not wait for late arrivals.

On arrival at Preguiça, aluguers (shared minivans) wait at the port and run to Ribeira Brava and Tarrafal de São Nicolau. If you are continuing to Tarrafal, confirm with the driver before boarding.

Getting to São Nicolau: Ferry or Flight?

São Nicolau has a small domestic airport near Preguiça. CVSky operates flights from Praia and other islands, though schedules are limited. There is no international airport on São Nicolau.

The ferry is the more common option for travellers coming from São Vicente, since Mindelo has no direct flight to São Nicolau. The sea crossing is also significantly cheaper than flying from Praia. For most visitors already on São Vicente, the ferry is the natural choice.

Why People Take This Ferry

São Nicolau is widely considered the most authentic and least-visited of Cape Verde's inhabited islands. Travellers come for the Monte Gordo Natural Park, the volcanic landscapes, the dramatic ribeiras (valleys) carved through the mountains, and a way of life that has changed little in decades.

The island is home to some of the best hiking in the archipelago, rivalling Santo Antão but with far fewer visitors. The cultural heritage is deep — São Nicolau was historically the intellectual heart of Cape Verde, home to the country's first seminary and a strong literary tradition.

The Cultural Heart of Cape Verde

Ribeira Brava, the main town, has a quiet colonial centre with colourful houses, a seminary-turned-museum, and some of the friendliest people you'll meet in the islands. Tarrafal de São Nicolau, on the north coast, is a small fishing town with a black-sand beach and a handful of guesthouses.

The island produces some of the best grogue (Cape Verdean rum) in the archipelago and has a strong tradition of morna music. If you're looking for the Cape Verde that guidebooks describe but tourists rarely see, São Nicolau is it.

Other Routes From São Vicente

São Vicente is also the starting point for the daily ferry to Santo Antão (60 minutes, multiple daily crossings with both CV Interilhas and Nôs Ferry). The Mindelo to Praia route via the Redonda circuit connects São Vicente to Santiago and the southern islands.
