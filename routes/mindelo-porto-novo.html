Mindelo to Porto Novo Ferry

Search Mindelo → Porto Novo ferries arrow_forward

The ferry from Mindelo to Porto Novo is the most frequently searched crossing in Cape Verde. Mindelo is the cultural capital of São Vicente and Porto Novo is the entry port for Santo Antão, one of the most spectacular hiking destinations in the archipelago.

How Long Does the Mindelo to Porto Novo Ferry Take?

The crossing from Mindelo to Porto Novo takes between 50 and 60 minutes depending on sea conditions and the vessel. CV Interilhas operates the Chiquinho on this route, which typically completes the crossing in around 60 minutes. Nôs Ferry operates the Mar d'Canal on the same route with similar journey times.

How Much Does the Mindelo to Porto Novo Ferry Cost?

The fare from Mindelo to Porto Novo starts at 950 CVE for Cape Verde nationals and 1500 CVE for non-nationals, which is approximately €14. Both operators charge similar prices on this route. Tickets can be bought at the terminal in Mindelo or online through the CV Interilhas website.

How Often Does the Ferry Run?

This is the busiest route in Cape Verde with multiple sailings every day in both directions. CV Interilhas departs Mindelo at 07:00 and 14:00 daily. Nôs Ferry departs at 08:00 and 15:00 on weekdays and Saturdays, with slightly different times on Sundays. Use the Ondas search tool to see all available departures on your chosen date.

Which Operators Serve Mindelo to Porto Novo?

Two operators run this route daily making it the most competitive crossing in Cape Verde. CV Interilhas operates the Chiquinho vessel. Nôs Ferry operates the Mar d'Canal vessel. Both depart from the Cais Marítima terminal in Mindelo and arrive at Porto Novo on Santo Antão.

Where Do Ferries Depart From in Mindelo?

All ferries to Porto Novo depart from the Cais Marítima terminal in Mindelo. The terminal is located on the seafront and is easy to reach from the city centre on foot or by taxi. Arrive at least 30 minutes before departure to buy your ticket and board.

Why Is This Route So Important?

Santo Antão has no working airport so the Mindelo to Porto Novo ferry is the only way to reach the island. This makes it an essential crossing rather than an optional one for anyone visiting Santo Antão. The island is known for the Paul valley, the dramatic coast road and the village of Fontainhas perched on the cliffs above the sea.
