About CV Interilhas

CV Interilhas operates the largest fleet of inter-island ferries in Cape Verde. The company connects all 9 inhabited islands through three main service lines, using a fleet that includes the Chiquinho, Dona Tututa, and Liberdadi vessels.

The company is based in Mindelo, São Vicente, and its official website is cvinterilhas.cv. Tickets can be purchased online, at port offices, or through local travel agents across the islands.

CV Interilhas Routes

Linha Barlavento

The Barlavento line is the most frequent service, connecting São Vicente and Santo Antão with daily crossings. The vessel Chiquinho operates this route with morning and afternoon departures from Mindelo and return crossings from Porto Novo — exact times change with each timetable, so check the live schedule before you travel. The crossing takes approximately 60 minutes.

Linha Sotavento

The Sotavento line connects the southern islands using the vessel Liberdadi. It serves Santiago, Fogo, Brava and Maio on a rotating weekly schedule. Santiago to Fogo takes approximately 4 hours. Santiago to Maio takes approximately 2 hours. The Fogo to Brava crossing is the shortest at around 1 hour.

Linha Redonda

The Redonda line is the longest route, connecting São Vicente with São Nicolau, Sal, Boa Vista and Santiago using the Dona Tututa. This circular route operates twice weekly and journey times range from 3 hours for the shortest leg to 19 hours for the full São Vicente to Santiago crossing.

Buying CV Interilhas Tickets

There are three ways to buy CV Interilhas ferry tickets. The most convenient is through the official website at cvinterilhas.cv/buy, where you can select your route, date and number of passengers. Payment is made online and you receive an electronic ticket.

The second option is buying directly at ferry terminal ticket offices. Each port has a counter that typically opens 2 hours before departure. For the Barlavento line between São Vicente and Santo Antão, tickets are almost always available on the day. For the Sotavento and Redonda lines, availability is more limited.

The third option is through authorised local travel agents on each island. This can be useful if you prefer personal assistance or do not have internet access.

There are two price tiers: a national rate for Cape Verdean residents and a non-national rate for visitors. The non-national rate is typically around 60 percent higher. Prices shown on Ondas are the non-national fare unless noted.

Tips for buying tickets: For longer routes such as Praia to Fogo or the Linha Redonda, book in advance — these routes operate only a few days per week and can sell out during holiday periods, especially Easter and summer. For the São Vicente to Santo Antão crossing, you can usually buy on the day, but weekends and public holidays are safer with advance booking.

Arrive at the port at least 90 minutes before departure for long routes and at least 30 minutes for the São Vicente to Santo Antão crossing. Boarding closes before the scheduled departure time and the ferry will not wait for late passengers.

Ferry services can be disrupted by adverse sea conditions, particularly during the windier months from November to March. When the Instituto Nacional de Meteorologia e Geofísica (INMG) issues weather warnings, CV Interilhas may delay or cancel sailings.

The Redonda and longer Sotavento routes are most susceptible to weather-related disruptions. The short Barlavento crossing between São Vicente and Santo Antão is the most reliable and rarely gets cancelled. Always check close to your travel date for the latest status.

Common Questions About CV Interilhas

Does CV Interilhas connect all 9 islands?

Yes. Through its three service lines (Barlavento, Sotavento and Redonda), CV Interilhas provides connections to all 9 inhabited islands of Cape Verde: São Vicente, Santo Antão, São Nicolau, Sal, Boa Vista, Santiago, Maio, Fogo and Brava.

How far in advance can I book a CV Interilhas ticket?

Tickets typically become available for purchase once the seasonal schedule is published. For popular routes, booking a few days ahead is advisable. For the Barlavento line you can often buy at the port on the day.

What vessels does CV Interilhas operate?

CV Interilhas currently operates three main vessels: the Chiquinho on the Barlavento line, the Liberdadi on the Sotavento line, and the Dona Tututa on the Redonda line.

Is there a difference in price for nationals and non-nationals?

Yes. CV Interilhas offers a reduced fare for Cape Verdean nationals and residents. The non-national fare is approximately 60 percent higher. You may be asked to show identification when boarding.

Can I take luggage on a CV Interilhas ferry?

Yes. Passengers can bring luggage onboard. Large items and excess baggage may incur an additional charge. Vehicles can also be transported on certain routes — check with the operator for availability and pricing.
