Nôs Ferry — Mindelo to Porto Novo

Search Mindelo → Porto Novo ferries arrow_forward

Nôs Ferry is one of two ferry operators in Cabo Verde, focused exclusively on the Mindelo–Porto Novo crossing between São Vicente and Santo Antão. The company is known for reliability on one of the busiest inter-island routes in the archipelago. For live departure times, search the Mindelo to Porto Novo route on Ondas.

The Mindelo to Porto Novo Route

The crossing connects Mindelo port on São Vicente with Porto Novo port on Santo Antão. Porto Novo is the gateway for visitors heading to Santo Antão — an island known for its dramatic mountain valleys, hiking trails and the village of Fontainhas perched above the sea.

The journey takes approximately 1 hour across the open Atlantic channel between the two islands. Nôs Ferry runs two crossings in each direction every day, including Sundays, making it one of the most frequent ferry services in Cabo Verde.

Route details

  • Origin: Mindelo, São Vicente
  • Destination: Porto Novo, Santo Antão
  • Both directions: Mindelo → Porto Novo and Porto Novo → Mindelo
  • Islands connected: São Vicente ↔ Santo Antão
  • Frequency: Two crossings each way, daily including Sundays
  • Duration: Approximately 1 hour

For current departure times, search the route on Ondas: Mindelo → Porto Novo · Porto Novo → Mindelo.

Tickets and Practical Info

Tickets can be booked online at the official Nôs Ferry website or bought in person at the ticket offices in the Gare Marítima of Mindelo and Porto Novo. Ticket offices are open Monday to Saturday 07:00–18:00 and Sunday 08:00–17:00.

Exact times and prices should be confirmed via the Ondas search or directly with the operator, as they may change seasonally. During high season and holiday periods it is advisable to buy tickets in advance.

Onboard amenities include family and group seating areas, food and bar service, vehicle and cargo transport, and assistance for passengers with reduced mobility (with advance notice to the operator).

Frequently Asked Questions

What route does Nôs Ferry operate?

Nôs Ferry operates the Mindelo to Porto Novo route, connecting São Vicente and Santo Antão. It is the only route Nôs Ferry serves, running two crossings each direction every day including Sundays.

How long is the Mindelo to Porto Novo crossing?

The crossing between Mindelo and Porto Novo with Nôs Ferry takes approximately 1 hour in each direction.

How often does Nôs Ferry run?

Nôs Ferry runs two crossings in each direction every day, including Sundays. For exact departure times, search the route on Ondas or check the official Nôs Ferry website.

Where do I buy Nôs Ferry tickets?

Tickets can be purchased online at nosferry.com or in person at the ticket offices in the Gare Marítima of Mindelo and Porto Novo. Ticket offices are open Monday to Saturday 07:00–18:00 and Sunday 08:00–17:00.

What is the difference between Nôs Ferry and CV Interilhas?

Both operators serve the Mindelo to Porto Novo route between São Vicente and Santo Antão. Nôs Ferry focuses exclusively on this single crossing, while CV Interilhas operates routes connecting all 9 islands of Cabo Verde. Departure times differ between the two operators, so it is worth checking both to find the sailing that suits you. Ondas shows results from both operators in one search.
